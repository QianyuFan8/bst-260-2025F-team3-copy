Risk Factors for Diabetes Among AgeAdults Aged 40 and Above: 
A Combined Logistic Regression and Random Forest Analysis Using NHANES Data


Research Questions:
1. Among U.S. adults aged 40 years and older, how do socioeconomic, behavioral, and psychosocial factors influence the risk of diabetes?
2. How do the variable importance rankings from a random forest model compare with the statistically significant predictors from logistic regression?


Analysis Plan:
1. Data Preparation
* Subset NHANES to participants aged 40+
* Outcome variable: Diabetes status(binary)
* Clean missing data (exclude or impute)
* Exclude pregnant women
* Normalize or categorize continuous predictors 
2. EDA
* Summarize all predictors by diabetes status
* Visualize distributions (e.g. histogram, boxplots)
3. Model 1 - Logistic regression
* Assess significant predictors (p-value, odds ratio, 95% CI)
* model fit and possible interaction terms
4. Model 2 - Random Forest
* Need to consider imbalance data 
* Evaluate feature importance (SHAP)
* Model performance (PR-AUC, accuracy, confusion matrix)
* Compare feature importance with logistic regression
5. Model Comparison and interpretation
* Identify overlap and differences in important predictors between methods
* Discuss interpretability vs predictive accuracy
* Relate findings to existing literature
